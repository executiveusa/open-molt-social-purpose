## The Deliverable: Executive Funding Map

At the end of 30 days, Grant Scout delivers one document:

**EXECUTIVE FUNDING MAP**

For each of 15+ objectively qualified opportunities:
- Eligibility evidence (against your criteria, confirmed from primary source)
- Deadline calendar
- Funding range
- Program area fit rationale
- Risk / effort estimate
- Next-action recommendation
- Source link + verification status

Plus:
- **3–5 priority recommendations** — ranked by fit, deadline urgency, and effort required
- **Excluded list** — everything screened out and why (so you never re-check it)

This is not a spreadsheet of links. This is a decision-ready brief.

## How Qualification Works

**Discovery hierarchy** (four distinct layers):

```
DISCOVERED
Raw candidates from federal, state, foundation, and corporate sources

      ↓ objective qualification rules (agreed before mission starts)

QUALIFIED
Meets all pre-agreed criteria — these are counted for contract purposes

      ↓ client review

SHORTLISTED
Client confirms strategic relevance to current priorities

      ↓ optional follow-on mission

PURSUED
Application or relationship work actually begins
```

**A QUALIFIED grant must meet ALL of the following** (criteria are locked before work starts — neither Grant Scout nor the client may change them after AUTHORIZED):

| Criterion | Standard |
|-----------|----------|
| Eligible geography | Matches your service area |
| Eligible entity type | 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor confirmed |
| Relevant program area | Matches your stated mission categories |
| Open deadline | Deadline ≥ agreed lead time (default: 45 days) |
| Award size | ≥ agreed minimum (you set this at intake) |
| Currently open or confirmed upcoming | Verified from funder's primary source — no grant-blog speculation |
| Primary source verified | Linked directly to funder website or official RFP |

If a grant does not meet every criterion, it does not count toward QUALIFIED. Grant Scout cannot reclassify downward after delivery.

## What Grant Scout Will and Will Not Do

**Authorized (what Grant Scout may do):**
- Search federal, state, foundation, and corporate grant databases
- Screen eligibility against your confirmed criteria
- Verify from primary sources
- Draft the Executive Funding Map
- Flag opportunities for human review before finalizing
- Deliver the map in a format you specify (PDF, Notion, Google Doc)

**Out of scope (hard stops — requires explicit new authorization):**
- Submit any application on your behalf
- Make financial commitments
- Contact funders directly
- Handle donor data, health data, or legal documents
- Do anything not in the signed Authorized Mission Scope

Every significant action is logged. You can read every step in plain English.
